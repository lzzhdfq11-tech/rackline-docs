Product page blocks and the variant order table

The Rackline product page is built from theme blocks inside the Product information section, so you can reorder, remove or add blocks in the theme editor. Besides the usual title, price and buy buttons, it has blocks for SKU, stock, a variant order table, specifications, downloads, pickup availability and a delivery note, plus app blocks.

Product information section

The Product information section holds the media gallery on one side and the product blocks on the other. Section settings: Media column width, Media position on desktop, Keep product information in view while scrolling, color scheme and spacing. The Featured product section uses the same blocks, so you can show a product with the same buying controls on any page.

The default product template shows these blocks in this order: Title, SKU, Price, Stock status, Variant picker, Buy buttons, Pickup availability, Variant order table, Specifications, Description, Downloads, Collapsible row, Complementary products and Share. The Related products section follows below.

Content blocks such as Heading, Text, Button, Image, Group and Custom Liquid, and app blocks from your installed apps, can also be added to the product page.

Variant order table

The variant order table (variant matrix) lists every variant of a product as a row: option values, SKU, stock, price with compact volume pricing, and a quantity field that starts at 0. Buyers fill in quantities for the variants they need; a footer that stays in view shows the number of selected lines, the subtotal and an Add selected to cart button that adds all of them at once.

  • Quantity rules apply to each row, and rows with a quantity are marked as picked.
  • Keyboard: Enter moves to the next row, Shift+Enter to the previous row, Ctrl+Enter adds the selected rows.
  • Theme settings → Pricing and trade → Maximum rows in the variant order table (25 to 250, default 100) limits long tables; beyond it the table shows the first rows and a link to all options.
  • The same table opens from the Options button on collection and search rows. See Collections and search.

Specifications

The Specifications block shows a two-column list. Built-in rows show the selected variant’s SKU, barcode and weight, the brand (linked to all products of that vendor) and the product type. Add up to 8 rows of your own: type a label such as “Voltage” and connect the value to a product metafield with the dynamic source button. Empty rows are hidden, and the list updates when the variant changes.

Downloads

For data sheets, manuals or certificates, create a product metafield of type List of files (for example custom.documents), upload the files to it for each product, and enter the namespace and key in the Downloads block. You can also add up to three fixed links, such as a general catalog.

Stock display

Stock follows Theme settings → Stock: hidden, “In stock / low stock”, or the exact quantity. A variant counts as low stock at or below the Low stock threshold. Only products whose inventory Shopify tracks show low stock or a count. The Stock status block can also show an incoming stock note, with the expected date when Shopify has one. Rackline does not show urgency messages or invented numbers.

When prices are hidden from guests

If Show prices to is set to signed-in customers only, guests see a “Trade prices” panel with a sign-in link instead of prices, quantity fields and buy buttons. See Price visibility.

Related products

The Related products section shows Shopify product recommendations below the product, as a table or a grid. Settings: heading, maximum items, layout, columns on desktop, show brand, show SKU and show stock status. Complementary products (the block) come from Shopify’s Search & Discovery app, where you choose which products pair with each product.
