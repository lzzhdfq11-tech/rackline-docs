Rackline: a Shopify theme for B2B trade suppliers

Rackline is a Shopify theme for wholesale and trade suppliers whose buyers reorder by SKU. It shows Shopify’s native B2B company prices, quantity rules and volume pricing, and adds a quick order page, table-first collections, a variant order table and a cart built for purchase orders. It comes with three presets: Rackline, Ledger and Crate.

Who Rackline is for

Rackline is designed for stores where most orders come from business buyers who already know what they need:

  • Distributors and wholesalers that sell to trade accounts with company prices and payment terms set up in Shopify B2B.
  • Suppliers whose buyers order by SKU or part number, often many lines at once, and reorder the same items.
  • Catalogs with many variants per product (sizes, pack quantities, colors) where buyers order several variants together.
  • Stores that sell to both trade and retail customers, or that show prices only to signed-in customers.

Key B2B workflows

Company context

Signed-in B2B buyers see their company and location in the header, with a link to switch location in their customer account.

Company prices

Prices from the buyer’s B2B catalog appear on product pages, lists, search suggestions, quick order and the cart.

Quantity rules

Minimum, maximum and increment rules are applied to every quantity field. Typed quantities snap to a valid value with a message.

Price ladders

Volume pricing appears as a ladder of price breaks with the current tier highlighted, the saving and a live subtotal.

Quick order

Buyers type or scan SKUs, paste lines from a spreadsheet or upload a CSV file. Each line is checked for price, stock and quantity rules before it goes to the cart.

Table view

Collections and search results list products as rows with stock, price and a quantity field plus Add on every row.

Variant order table

Order many variants of one product at once from a table with a running subtotal and one Add selected to cart button.

Cart for purchase orders

Order notes, a purchase order number field, CSV download of the cart and a request-a-quote form.

Price visibility

Show prices to everyone, or only to signed-in customers while guests see a sign-in link.

How a reorder works

  1. 01 Enter linesThe buyer types or scans SKUs, pastes lines from a spreadsheet or uploads a CSV file on the quick order page.
  2. 02 CheckEach line shows the product, the buyer’s price, stock and the quantity rule. Lines that are not found are flagged.
  3. 03 Add allOne button adds every found line to the cart, with quantities adjusted to the quantity rules.
  4. 04 ReviewOn the Order review page the buyer adds a PO number and a note, downloads a CSV copy or requests a quote, then checks out.
