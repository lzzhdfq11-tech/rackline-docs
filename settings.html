Theme settings reference

Theme settings in Rackline apply to the whole store. Open them in the theme editor under Theme settings. This page lists every settings group with its options; defaults are the Rackline preset values, and the Ledger and Crate presets change some of them.

Visual style

Visual style
SettingOptionsDefault
Visual styleBin labels, Ledger, Crate labelsBin labels

Sets the graphic language used on every template: cards, tables, price breaks, navigation and section headings. See Presets.

Colors

Color schemes are a group of five schemes in each preset; you can add more. Each scheme has these colors:

Social media

Links for LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, Pinterest, Vimeo, Tumblr and Snapchat. They appear in the Social links block and in the Organization structured data on the home page; the X link also sets the twitter:site tag.
