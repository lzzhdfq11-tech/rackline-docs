Changelog

Release notes for the Rackline Shopify theme. The theme version is shown in Online Store → Themes under the theme name.

1.0.0: Initial release

Release date: October 2026

Presets

  • Rackline (warehouse bin labels), Ledger (account book) and Crate (market case labels), each with its own visual style, fonts, color schemes, density and home page layout.

B2B

  • Company and location chip in the header and menu drawer for B2B buyers.
  • Company prices from Shopify B2B catalogs on every page.
  • Quantity rules on every quantity field, with hints and snapping to valid quantities.
  • Price ladder for volume pricing on product pages, and an optional compact ladder in lists.
  • Price visibility setting (everyone or signed-in customers only) and a tax note next to prices.
  • Trade welcome section and a trade account application template.

Ordering

  • Quick order template: SKU and barcode lines, paste from a spreadsheet, CSV upload, sample CSV and an order check.
  • Table and grid views for collections and search, remembered per shopper.
  • Variant order table on product pages and in the Options dialog from lists.
  • Predictive search that matches SKUs and barcodes.
  • Cart side panel or Order review page with order notes, PO number (optional or required), cart CSV download and quote requests.

Catalog and content

  • Product blocks for SKU, stock, specifications, downloads, pickup availability, delivery note and complementary products.
  • Aisle codes from the custom.zone_code collection metafield on menus, collection lists and product labels.
  • Brand index with an A–Z vendor directory, blog, FAQ, contact, 404 and password templates.
  • Structured data for Organization, WebSite, Product or ProductGroup, BreadcrumbList, BlogPosting and FAQPage.
