Support

Partwise Studio supports the Rackline Shopify theme through a support form. We reply within two business days. Support covers bug fixes and questions about the theme’s settings, sections and features; it does not include custom development.

Contact support

Send your request through the support form. We reply within two business days.

Open the support form

What support covers

  • Bug fixes: anything in the theme that does not work as documented. Fixes are released in theme updates.
  • Theme questions: how to use settings, sections and blocks, the quick order page, price ladders, aisle codes and the other features in this documentation.
  • Setup guidance: which theme settings to use for your B2B setup, and where in Shopify a feature is configured.

What support does not cover

  • Custom development: new features, design changes or code changes made for your store.
  • Problems caused by third-party apps or by edits to the theme code.
  • Setting up your Shopify B2B data, such as companies, catalogs and payment terms. Shopify’s Help Center covers these.

Before you contact us

  • Check the FAQ and the guide for the feature, for example Quick order or B2B pricing.
  • Check whether the problem also happens with apps turned off or in an unedited copy of the theme.
  • Make sure you use the latest Rackline version (see the changelog).

What to include

  • Your store address.
  • The theme version and the preset you started from (Rackline, Ledger or Crate).
  • The page where it happens and the steps to reproduce it.
  • Whether you were signed in as a B2B customer, a regular customer or a guest.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording, and the browser and device.

Collaborator access

For some issues we may ask for collaborator access so we can look at your theme settings. You approve the request in your Shopify admin, and you can remove access at any time. We ask only for theme permissions, work on a copy of your theme, and never ask for your password.
