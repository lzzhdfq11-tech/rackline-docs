Quick order by SKU, barcode or CSV

The Rackline quick order page lets buyers build an order from SKUs instead of browsing. They type or scan SKUs or barcodes, paste lines from a spreadsheet or upload a CSV file; each line is checked against your catalog for the buyer’s price, stock and quantity rules, and one button adds everything to the cart.

Set up the page

  1. Create a page with the theme template order-pad (Online Store → Pages).
  2. Select it in Theme settings → Pricing and trade → Quick order page. The header Quick order button, the mobile menu and the Trade welcome section link to it.
  3. Adjust the Quick order section in the theme editor.

Enter SKUs line by line

Each line has a SKU field and a quantity field. Buyers type a SKU or barcode and the line is looked up after a short pause. Enter moves to the next field, so a barcode scanner that types the code like a keyboard works in the SKU field. Pasting a column of SKUs into one SKU field fills several lines.

A found line shows:

  • the product title, variant and SKU or barcode, and optionally an image;
  • stock status, following the theme’s stock settings;
  • the buyer’s unit price, including the volume pricing tier that applies (for example “50+ price”);
  • the line total.

Matching is by exact SKU or barcode, not case-sensitive. Products must be published to your online store and searchable, and B2B buyers only find products in their catalog.

Paste lines from a spreadsheet

Under Paste lines, buyers paste one item per line: the SKU or barcode, then the quantity, separated by a comma, tab or space. Copying two columns from a spreadsheet works because spreadsheet cells are separated by tabs. A first line such as SKU, Quantity is treated as a header. Repeated SKUs are combined into one line.

SKU-1001, 10
SKU-1002	24
SKU-2040 5

CSV format

Under Upload CSV, buyers choose a CSV file. The file is read on the buyer’s device and is not uploaded to a server.

  • Column 1: SKU or barcode.
  • Column 2: quantity. If the quantity is empty, the line keeps its default quantity, adjusted to the quantity rule.
  • A header row is detected automatically.
  • Commas, semicolons or tabs can separate the columns; quoted fields are supported.
  • Maximum file size: 1 MB. The order pad holds up to 250 lines.

The Download sample CSV link creates quick-order-sample.csv with this content:

sku,quantity
SKU-1001,10
SKU-1002,24
SKU-2040,5

Order check and subtotal

A summary panel counts the lines, how many were found and not found, how many quantities were adjusted and how many lines are sold out. It lists each issue by line number and shows an estimated subtotal that includes volume pricing. Discounts, shipping and taxes are shown in the cart and at checkout.

Add all to cart

Add all to cart sends every found line to the cart in one request. Lines that could not be added stay marked with the reason. Depending on the cart setting, the cart side panel opens or the buyer goes to the Order review page. Clear order pad empties all lines.

Tablets and phones

On screens narrower than 990 pixels, each line becomes a card with the SKU, product, quantity and total stacked, so the page works without horizontal scrolling on tablets and phones.

Sign-in and accessibility

  • When Show prices to is set to signed-in customers only, guests see “Sign in to use quick order” instead of the order pad.
  • Every field has a label that includes its line number, and results such as “Line 3: no product found” are announced to screen readers.
  • Quick order needs JavaScript; without it, buyers see a message and can add products from their pages.
