SEO, meta tags and structured data

Rackline outputs the meta tags and structured data that search engines and AI answer engines use to understand a store: a title, description and canonical link on every page, Open Graph and X (Twitter) tags, and JSON-LD for the organization, website search, products, breadcrumbs, articles and FAQs. Shopify itself generates the store’s sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

Prices for signed-in customers only

When Show prices to is set to signed-in customers only, product structured data leaves out offers, so search results do not show prices that guests cannot see on the page.

Page structure

  • One h1 per page, with section headings below it.
  • Images use the alt text you enter in Shopify; purely decorative images get an empty alt attribute.
  • Data tables use captions and column headers, which helps both screen readers and machines read product tables.
  • Collection and search pages can show breadcrumbs (section setting), and product and collection pages output breadcrumb structured data.
  • Pages are server-rendered Liquid, so content is readable without running JavaScript.

What you control

  • Fill in Theme settings → Logo and business: legal business name, sales phone and sales email.
  • Add your social media profiles in Theme settings → Social media.
  • Write a search engine title and description for your home page, collections, products and pages in your Shopify admin.
  • Give every product a SKU and, where you have one, a barcode (GTIN).
  • Use the Specifications block with metafields so key attributes are on the page as text.
  • Write collection descriptions and turn on Show collection description.
  • Add alt text to product and section images.
  • Answer common buyer questions in an FAQ section with structured data turned on.

Search engines and AI answer engines

AI answer engines read the same signals as search engines: clear page text, headings, tables and structured data. Product pages that state the SKU, specifications, pack sizes and stock as text, and an FAQ that answers trade questions (minimum order, delivery, payment terms), give these engines facts they can quote. Rackline does not hide product content behind scripts, and its tables use real table markup.
