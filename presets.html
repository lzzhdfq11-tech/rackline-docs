Rackline, Ledger and Crate presets

Rackline has three presets: Rackline (warehouse bin labels), Ledger (an account book) and Crate (market case labels). Each preset sets a visual style, fonts, color schemes, density, corner radius and a home page layout. All three include the same sections, blocks and B2B features, so the choice is about how your store looks, not what it can do.

Rackline: warehouse bin labels

The Rackline preset borrows everything from a parts warehouse: shelf bin labels, aisle signs with zone colors, floor-marking tape, label-printer type and the yellow of safety signage. Products appear as bin labels with the SKU as the loudest text, categories as aisle signs with a code such as B2, the variant order table and cart as a pick list with tick boxes, and section titles carry a hazard-stripe marker. Corners are square and there are no soft shadows. Density is compact so buyers see more rows.

Ledger: the account book

The Ledger preset looks like a bound trade ledger: pale green ledger paper with faint ruled lines, a double red margin rule, navy ink and serif headings, with printed figures for every amount. Amount columns are bounded by red rules, totals carry a double underline, and volume pricing is a tier table whose current row is marked with a red edge. Categories carry index tabs. It suits general distributors of office, packaging or janitorial supplies.

Crate: market case labels

The Crate preset is set at the loading bay of a food and catering wholesaler: kraft board, printed case-end labels with rounded corners and a dashed inner border, round stickers for short facts and price tiers per case. Colors are navy, tomato red and leaf green on kraft and label paper. Collections open in grid view, and the Delivery note block is drawn as a cut-off card.

Switching and mixing presets

  • Change the visual style in Theme settings → Visual style: Bin labels, Ledger or Crate labels. It changes the graphic language on every template (cards, tables, price breaks, navigation and section titles) but not your fonts, colors or content.
  • Mix freely. Fonts, color schemes, density and corner radius are separate settings, so you can, for example, use the Ledger visual style with your own brand colors.
  • Start fresh with another preset by adding Rackline again from the Shopify Theme Store with that preset. It is added as a separate unpublished theme, so your current theme is not changed.

Customizing a preset

  • Color schemes: each preset has five color schemes. Every section that paints a background has a color scheme setting. Each scheme sets background, text, headings, secondary text, links and focus, primary and secondary buttons, borders and table rules, panels and table headers, sale, in stock and low stock colors.
  • Fonts: heading, body and data fonts come from Shopify’s font library. The data font is used for SKUs, prices, quantities and tables; a monospaced font keeps columns aligned.
  • Zone colors: set a color per collection with the custom.zone_color metafield, or let the visual style assign zone colors by aisle letter.
  • Layout: page width, side margin, density, section spacing, corner radius and motion.

The preset color schemes were checked so that text colors reach at least 4.5:1 contrast on their backgrounds. If you change colors, check contrast again.
