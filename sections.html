Sections and blocks reference

This page lists every section, block and template in the Rackline Shopify theme with a one-line purpose. Most content sections accept theme blocks, so you can add headings, text, buttons, images and groups inside them, and many accept app blocks.

Search suggestions, Quick order lookup and Cart count are technical sections that the theme’s scripts load; they have no content to edit.

Header and footer

Header and footer sections
SectionPurpose
Utility barShort messages, a link menu, a trade application link and country/region and language selectors
HeaderLogo, search, main menu (mega menu), account, company chip, quick order button and cart; two desktop layouts and a sticky option
FooterColumns of blocks: menus, text, newsletter, social links, payment icons, Follow on Shop and selectors

The country/region and language selectors appear only when your store sells in more than one country or language.
